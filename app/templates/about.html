About & safety

Licensing. Transmitting requires an appropriate license for the frequencies you use — for example an amateur radio license on amateur bands, or a license for your own business/GMRS-style allocation where digital modes are permitted. You are responsible for station identification, coordination and staying within your license.

DVMProject terms. dvmhost/dvmfne are provided for personal, non-commercial, hobbyist use. They must never be used in public-safety or life-safety critical applications. See the project's usage guidelines.

This app never keys your transmitter by itself. It only writes configuration, starts/stops services when you click, and reads status. It does not call any dvmhost API that transmits (paging, grants, raw TSBK, mode changes).
